# M5 prose check: front matter, back matter and supplement

`tools/prose_diff.py` (3-gram windows of the OCR, `drafts/pNNN.txt`, against `pdftotext build/main.pdf`) for
the three new ranges of `inventory/chapters.json`. `front` and `back` run in `make all`; `supplement` has no
"units", so it is run explicitly: `python3 tools/prose_diff.py --chapter supplement` (reports in
`build/prose-front.md`, `build/prose-back.md`, `build/prose-supplement.md`). Every non-pass page was compared
with its scan page (`figures/pages/pNNN.png`, zoomed where needed) and with the compiled text. Skipped pages
(fewer than 30 OCR tokens) were checked too: every OCR token of 4+ letters was looked up in the compiled text,
and the pages with text were read against the scan.

**Result: no text is dropped, garbled or duplicated in these ranges; nothing needed fixing.** Three pages are
excused in `"artwork_pages"` of `"supplement"` with their reasons (665, 687, 712); with them the supplement run
exits 0.
