% Chapter 2, Figure 9: the linearization approximations for the rocket of Figs 7 and 8 (scan: figures/ch2/fig09.png).
% Curves: fig09.py. True M_c and M_d: the digitized curves of Figs 7 and 8 (fig07.py, fig08.py), the same data.
% Linear approximations: eq. (8) M_c = C_1 alpha_X and eq. (9) M_d = C_2 Omega_X, the tangents at zero, with the
% lettered C_1 = 1x10^6 dyn-cm and C_2 = 1.25x10^4 dyn-cm-sec. Limits of validity (dashed): 0.225 rad and
% 87 rad/sec (the caption). Both lines solid as printed. The true curve and its tangent coincide from the origin
% (to 0.20 rad and 60 rad/sec), so the tangent is drawn last and thinner, on top of the true curve, and shows as
% a line through the origin along the shared segment.
