\chapter{Vector Notation for Pressure Term of Rocket Thrust}\label{supp:vector}

In the parts of Chapters~\ref{ch1} and~\ref{ch4} that have been rewritten to account for the presence of a
pressure term in the equation for rocket thrust, that pressure term should be written
\begin{gather*}
(P_e - P_a)\vec{A}_e
\end{gather*}
rather than
\begin{gather*}
\overrightarrow{(P_e - P_a)A_e}
\end{gather*}
as I originally wrote it. On rereading these Chapter~\ref{ch1} and~\ref{ch4} corrections and additions, I
concluded that it is unnecessary and redundant to continue the vector arrow over the entire expression because
all the directional information necessary to define the pressure term as a vector quantity is carried by the
unit vector normal to the exhaust plane which makes it possible to write the exhaust plane area $\vec{A}_e$ as a
vector. The pressures $P_e$ and $P_a$ are intended to be regarded as essentially hydrostatic rather than as
components of a pressure tensor, and therefore convey no directional information.
