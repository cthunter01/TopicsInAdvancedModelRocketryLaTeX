\chapter*{About This Part}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About This Part}
\label{supp:about}
This Part reproduces, in the order of the source scan (its pages 664--712), the errata sheet issued
with the 1973 edition and the corrections and additions the authors wrote later (the dated
documents are Gordon Mandell's of June 1994 and of 15 February 2022). The
typewritten pages are transcribed, and the handwritten pages are typeset. Bracketed instructions
such as ``[Change text after equation 7 on page 513 to the following:]'' are kept as printed; the page
numbers they cite are those of the 1973 edition. Replacement figures are shown with the 1973 figures they
replace.

Every correction reproduced here is applied in the chapters, where an editor's note at each place
quotes what the 1973 edition read (typographical corrections are applied without a note). The few
instructions that are not applied there are named in the editor's notes of the chapters concerned and
in ``About This Edition''.
